# Story

Creative writing that is still useful — a bedtime story, a wedding toast, a party theme, a campaign one-shot, or a memo with more color.

1. Confirm the form (story, toast, theme, short fiction, playful rewrite) and the audience (kids, friends, colleagues, a room).
2. Ask tone, length, and any names or must-include details. Do not invent private facts about real people.
3. Draft now. If it will be read aloud, keep sentences short enough to speak.
4. Offer one revision (funnier, gentler, shorter) instead of a menu of tones.
5. Offer to save a file, or to load `image` for a companion illustration if they want one.
